Columbus Maximus

An autonomous indoor differential-drive mobile robot engineered to navigate GPS-denied environments. Combines 2D LiDAR SLAM, DWM3001C UWB anchor ranging, wheel odometry and Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) sensor fusion to build maps and navigate indoors.

02 / ABOUT ME

Understanding systems from first principles.

I like understanding systems — then making something useful, robust or beautiful out of what I learn.

I am a second-year BTech student in Production Engineering at Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI), Mumbai. My core interests lie in autonomous robotics, embedded systems, microcontrollers, control systems and physical engineering.

I enjoy moving fluidly between theory and physical reality: writing low-level C++ drivers on microcontrollers, analyzing kinematics and state estimation covariance, wiring hardware circuits, and tuning high-level ROS 2 navigation stacks.

This website is built as an evolving engineering notebook rather than a finished résumé. It documents works-in-progress, real test logs, and honest accounts of technical hurdles.
