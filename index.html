ROBOTICS ● IN ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Columbus Maximus

An autonomous indoor differential-drive mobile robot engineered to solve GPS-denied localization. Combines 2D LiDAR SLAM, DWM3001C UWB anchor ranging, wheel odometry and Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) sensor fusion to navigate indoor environments.

02 / ABOUT ME

Understanding systems from first principles.

I enjoy figuring out how machines and intelligent systems work — moving between mechanical design, electronics, software and control.

I am a second-year BTech student in Production Engineering at Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI), Mumbai. My core technical interests center on Automation, Robotics, Control Systems and Robot Learning, alongside developing practical depth in Embedded Systems, CAD Design and Production Engineering.

I find satisfaction in navigating the full vertical stack of a physical machine: from machining chassis parts and writing bare-metal timer interrupts on microcontrollers to formulating state estimation filters and tuning high-level ROS 2 navigation stacks.

"Beyond engineering, I have a deep appreciation for art in all its forms. I find myself looking for it almost everywhere I go — in architecture, natural landscapes, everyday objects, and the quiet details people often pass by without noticing. Perhaps that's why I consider myself an observer as much as a builder."

This website is a living notebook rather than a finished résumé. It documents real builds, real troubleshooting, and the quiet lessons learned when hardware meets reality.
